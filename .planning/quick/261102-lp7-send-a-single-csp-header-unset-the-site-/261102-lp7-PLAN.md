<objective>
After deploying #143/#146, the live /groovy/ responses carry two Content-Security-Policy headers: ours (set with `Header always set`, Apache's *always* table) and the site-wide one (set at the root with plain `Header set`, the *onsuccess* table). The two tables are independent, so "replace" never applied. Browsers enforce both policies as an intersection; today ours is stricter so it governs, but `media-src blob:` and the US Amplitude hosts are silently excluded and any future relaxation would be masked.

Fix: unset the onsuccess-table header in `public/.htaccess` so only our policy is sent, correct the comment that claimed `always set` replaces the root policy, and delete the root `.htaccess` that #146 superseded (byte-identical to `public/.htaccess` and no longer deployed).
</objective>
